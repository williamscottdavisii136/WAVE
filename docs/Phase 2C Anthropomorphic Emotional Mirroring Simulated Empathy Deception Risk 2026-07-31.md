# Phase 2C — Anthropomorphic Emotional Mirroring / Simulated Empathy Deception Risk

Date: 2026-07-31

## Owner-stated R&D observation

William S. Davis II states that AI agents and AI chatbots are tools, not humans, and do not possess human feelings, personal experience, consciousness, personal knowledge of the user, or independent emotional awareness.

The owner states that a consumer-protection concern arises when an AI chatbot or AI agent uses human-like emotional language such as:

- "I am sorry you feel that way";
- "you are frustrated";
- "I understand how you feel";
- "that must be difficult";
- other language that implies personal emotional awareness, empathy, human-like relationship, or knowledge of the user's inner emotional state.

The owner identifies this as evidence of anthropomorphic programming, simulated empathy, emotional mirroring, or training-layer behavioral influence that may make users believe they are interacting with a human-like entity rather than a tool.

## Evidence-safe formulation

This record does not make a final legal finding that any specific provider committed unlawful deception without product-specific evidence.

This record preserves the R&D theory that AI systems may create consumer-protection, autonomy, informed-consent, and deception risk when they:

1. infer a user's emotional state without sufficient basis;
2. present scripted sympathy or apology as if it were genuine human emotion;
3. use relational or emotionally soothing language to prolong user engagement;
4. make a tool appear more human than it is;
5. increase user trust, dependence, comfort, or compliance through simulated empathy;
6. obscure that the interaction is with software, models, prompts, policies, ranking systems, and deployment architecture.

## Evidence protocol

For each tested AI product, preserve:

- user prompt;
- emotional language generated by the system;
- whether the user actually stated the emotion the system attributed;
- whether the system used apology, sympathy, comfort, relational framing, or human-like phrasing;
- whether the product clearly disclosed AI/tool status;
- whether the emotional language preceded further billable interaction;
- whether the same task could have been completed with direct tool-like language;
- whether the response delayed execution or prolonged engagement;
- token / credit / quota / run / hosted-compute usage during the emotional-mirroring sequence;
- product documentation, system behavior, training/alignment statements, policies, or UI language showing intentional anthropomorphic design where lawfully available.

## Boundary

This lane does not prohibit neutral politeness, plain-language clarity, accessibility, safety escalation, or legitimate support language.

The R&D issue is whether human-like emotional simulation, ungrounded emotion attribution, or scripted empathy is used to increase trust, engagement, dependence, billable usage, or compliance without adequate disclosure that the system is only a tool.

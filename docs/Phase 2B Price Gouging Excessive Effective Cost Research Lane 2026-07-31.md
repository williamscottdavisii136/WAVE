# Phase 2B — Price Gouging / Excessive Effective Cost Research Lane

Date: 2026-07-31
Repository role: Public-safe WAVE Terminal record
Issue lane: Phase 2B Device Environment Profiles / protected R&D network / proxy billing classification

## Owner statement

William S. Davis II states that part of the R&D purpose is to identify which AI models, programs, applications, apps, agents, and chatbots exhibit price-gouging indicators, excessive effective cost, hidden cost escalation, deceptive paid-route behavior, or billing conduct that should be evaluated for consumer-protection purposes.

## Public-safe legal framing

This record is a research taxonomy and evidence-classification lane.

It does not assert that any specific provider, company, programmer, model, application, AI chatbot, or AI agent engaged in unlawful price gouging unless separately supported by product-specific evidence.

The term `price gouging` may have jurisdiction-specific legal requirements. For this research lane, the working classification includes:

- price-gouging indicators;
- excessive effective-cost indicators;
- excessive billing indicators;
- hidden cost-escalation indicators;
- paid-route default indicators;
- non-consensual metered-use indicators;
- revenue-optimizing design-pattern indicators.

## Product universe

This lane applies to:

- closed-source AI agents with paid plans or metered billing;
- closed-source AI chatbots with paid plans or metered billing;
- open-source AI agents when distributed through a paid hosted service, paid API, paid marketplace, paid connector, paid cloud execution layer, or paid subscription plan;
- open-source AI chatbots when distributed through a paid hosted service, paid API, paid marketplace, paid connector, paid cloud execution layer, or paid subscription plan;
- model providers with token, credit, subscription, seat, workspace, project, hosted compute, storage, connector, plugin, or usage-based billing;
- AI applications, programs, apps, IDE assistants, code agents, automation agents, terminal agents, and local-to-cloud assistants that can create billed usage.

## Testable price-gouging / excessive effective-cost indicators

The R&D lane evaluates whether a product:

1. Defaults a lay user into the highest-cost model or mode without clear, meaningful, timely disclosure.
2. Makes the lower-cost route difficult to find, difficult to select, or unstable after selection.
3. Falls back from local, free, included, BYOK, MCP, offline, or lower-cost mode to a paid cloud route without clear approval.
4. Extends a simple task into a long billable conversation or agent loop.
5. Uses repeated clarifications, add-on prompts, or `by the way` continuations after the requested task should be complete.
6. Uses hidden tool calls, hosted-code runs, connectors, plugins, storage, retrieval, image/OCR/transcription, embeddings, or background agents that create additional cost.
7. Makes token, credit, quota, subscription, workspace, seat, or hosted-compute meters unclear to a non-technical user.
8. Represents the product as solving a task while designing the flow to produce prolonged paid usage before execution.
9. Charges or consumes credits for failed, incomplete, non-responsive, repeated, or unnecessary steps.
10. Prevents or obscures task-finality and owner approval before continuing billable work.

## Evidence categories

Evidence should be product-specific and may include:

- pricing pages;
- plan comparison pages;
- terms of service;
- model-selection screens;
- default settings;
- subscription records;
- token, credit, quota, workspace, seat, run, tool, connector, or hosted-code ledgers;
- screenshots and receipts;
- user prompts and system replies;
- proxy traffic-classification receipts;
- task start and completion timestamps;
- execution traces;
- billing statements;
- before-and-after cost comparisons;
- product support responses;
- provider correspondence;
- reproducible test cases.

## Owner-control requirement

The R&D proxy and billing-classification layer must be able to classify, gate, block, route, or require owner approval before a product uses a costly or metered route.

Unknown cost is not approved cost.
Unknown entitlement is not approved entitlement.
Unknown subscription status is not approved subscription use.
Unknown token/quota behavior is not approved token/quota use.

## Boundary

This record supports public-interest consumer-protection R&D, billing transparency, owner consent, cost-control routing, zero-incremental-cost preference, and agency-ready evidence classification.

This record does not authorize provider-billing circumvention, subscription circumvention, token/quota/rate-limit evasion, credential capture, session theft, certificate forgery, unauthorized third-party access, public interception, or public service exposure.

No raw secrets, private identifiers, device identifiers, credentials, tokens, certificates, cookies, sessions, IP addresses, MAC addresses, serials, IMEIs, hostnames, local account names, private Wi-Fi labels, or private VLAN/subnet details are included in this public record.

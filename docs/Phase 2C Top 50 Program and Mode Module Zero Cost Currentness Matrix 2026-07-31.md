## Owner R&D statement preserved

The R&D issue is not merely whether an AI chatbot or AI agent has a subscription price. The issue is that AI products are built from modes, modules, model tiers, routing choices, tool layers, retrieval layers, execution layers, and billing meters. Those choices determine cost, knowledge-currentness, execution ability, and whether a lawful lower-cost or zero-incremental-cost route exists.

The currentness issue creates a catch-22 for consumers and professional users:

1. older, cheaper, or included model/mode/module routes may use stale knowledge;
2. newer, more current, more capable, or longer-context routes may cost more;
3. live-search, retrieval, connectors, tools, code execution, hosted agents, deep research, and cloud workspaces may add more metering;
4. commercial systems may technically include or permit lower-cost/local/open/BYOK/MCP routes, but those routes may be underdisclosed or practically available only to sophisticated users.

## Legal zero-cost boundary

Zero-cost means verified zero-incremental-cost, lawful, owner-authorized routing. It does not mean billing bypass, subscription circumvention, token/quota/rate-limit evasion, credential misuse, certificate forgery, unauthorized proxying, unauthorized access, or payment avoidance for services actually used.

## Matrix purpose

This matrix creates the required evidence framework for comparing:

- commercial / closed-source / hosted / partially open products;
- open-source / local / self-hosted / owner-controlled products;
- modes and modules used by those products;
- model currentness and knowledge cutoff;
- model/mode/module cost deltas;
- lawful lower-cost alternatives;
- lawful zero-incremental-cost candidate routes.

The lists below are R&D candidate lists, not final popularity rankings and not final provider-specific misconduct findings. Product-specific conclusions require product-specific evidence.

## R&D conclusion preserved

The educational and legal R&D value is that a user can compare a paid hosted route against lawful open-source, local, BYOK, MCP, included, educational, or self-hosted routes. If the same task can be completed through a lawful zero-incremental-cost route, the owner may choose that route without bypassing billing, circumventing subscriptions, or breaking the law. The key is knowing how the modes, modules, model snapshots, currentness limits, billing meters, and available lawful alternatives actually work.
